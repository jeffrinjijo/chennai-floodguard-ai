Read this before using the numbers

  • The score ranks areas by recurrent flood-proneness in past records. It is not a probability and not a warning.
  • Models trained on other events score below chance on 2005 and 2015, so the map describes the pooled history, not any single storm.
  • Forecast rain comes from a public weather model that smooths extremes. Official alerts: IMD and the Greater Chennai Corporation.
